I enjoy working with materials and light.

I work on engineering light-matter interactions to achieve useful applications. Previously, I've worked with thread-based electronics and quantum dot photodetectors. Before that, I programmed in C & C++. I still have a deep interest in low level systems programming and the hardware in embedded systems, especially with Linux systems. Check out the Projects page for more details!

Ph.D. student at UW-Madison. Class of 2020, BITS, Pilani.

I'm currently a Ph.D. student at the ECE Department at UW Madison. I graduated with a B.E. in Electronics and Electrical Engineering, and an M.Sc. in Chemistry from BITS Pilani.

Absolutely fascinated by space.

Since my childhood, I've had a deep interest for all things space, whether it's astronomy, rockets, or satellites. Team Anant was my first experience with satellites. I worked on the On-Board Computer subsystem and founded the Publicity and Sponsorship Division. I interned at ISRO in 2018, where I worked on image processing techniques.

I dabble in music.

I was with Gurukul, the Department of Music at BITS Pilani as a drummer for 3 years where we performed in about 3 events every semester, and taught students through the Gurukul workshop. I now also play around with a guitar and FL Studio. Check out my work on SoundCloud.
